Privacy Policy

Clipper, the uploader for the @gta6clipedits accounts. Last updated November 14, 2026.

Who uses it

Clipper is a personal tool that runs on one computer owned by the person who runs the @gta6clipedits accounts. It is used only with that person's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses, and why

  • TikTok, with the account owner's permission: the account's open_id and display name (user.info.basic), only to check the connected account is the right one, and uploading videos to that account's TikTok drafts (video.upload). The owner finishes and posts each draft in the TikTok app.
  • Instagram (Meta), with the account owner's permission: publishing Reels to the owner's own Instagram account, and reading that account's own posts and their view, like and comment counts.
  • YouTube (Google), with the account owner's permission: uploading Shorts to the owner's own channel.

It does not read anyone else's account, messages, followers or contacts.

Where data is kept

Access tokens are stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. Videos, post links and view counts are stored in files on that same computer. There is no server and no outside database. Code backups never contain tokens or passwords.

Sharing

Data from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube is not sold, rented or shared, and TikTok data is not sent to any other service. The only things that leave the computer are the videos posted to the owner's own accounts, the public links of those posts (submitted to the clip campaigns they were made for), and notifications sent to the owner's own phone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in each platform's settings for connected apps. The owner can also delete the stored tokens from the Keychain, after which Clipper can no longer reach that platform.

Children

Clipper is not meant for, and does not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
